Executive verdict

The idea is possible. The specific demand still has to be measured.

Axora is a working MVP, not a visual prototype. The software can already support a controlled procurement process. The next job is to prove that three real companies use it repeatedly because it saves time and reduces manual work.

YesTechnical feasibilityA functioning system already exists.

ConditionalOperational readinessCOD controls, access and compliance must pass gates.
Not provenMalaysia-wide demandThree case studies cannot represent the whole market.

What the pilot can prove

  • Whether users understand and complete the workflow.
  • Whether requests, approvals, quotations, deliveries and COD records become easier.
  • Whether manual messages, spreadsheet entries and status chasing decrease.
  • Whether the operator can deliver a reliable and compliant service.
  • Whether the companies ask to continue after the test.

What the pilot cannot prove

  • The size of the Malaysian market.
  • That every industry has the same problem.
  • A national logistics or supplier network.
  • Profitable credit, warehousing or a public marketplace.
  • Long-term willingness to pay if the pilot itself is free.
Recommendation Run a six-week, invitation-only B2B pilot using cash on delivery only. Use real but capped transactions. Compare each company's baseline process with Axora. Continue only if at least two companies show repeat use and measurable improvement, and all COD, privacy and security gates pass.

Four evidence levels keep the decision honest

The report separates what is known from what still has to be learned. This prevents a working system from being mistaken for proven product-market fit.

Confirmed

Public facts

Official Malaysian sources, current competitor pages and Lawazem's public documentation.

Inspected

Axora build

Approved pilot scope

COD only

The live MVP will use cash on delivery only as a temporary safety boundary. Any electronic payment capability is outside the approved pilot scope and is not evidence of launch readiness or the final product vision.

To validate

Pilot evidence

Actual adoption, time savings, error reduction, satisfaction, continuation and willingness to pay.

The decision question

Do the three companies have a recurring procurement coordination problem that Axora solves better than their current method?

This is more precise than asking whether they "like the app" or whether the general idea sounds good.

The unit of evidence

A completed purchase cycle with timestamps, users, quotations, approval, delivery evidence, COD receipt evidence and outcome.

Opinions support the analysis, but real behavior is the primary evidence.

Six-week live pilot
10
Validation timeline

Run a short, measurable pilot with real work

Six weeks is long enough to observe repeated behavior. If any company has fewer than ten eligible purchases, extend that company until it reaches the minimum evidence volume.

Week 0PrepareContracts, access, COD procedure, backup and baseline data.
Week 1OnboardTrain roles and complete two guided transactions per company.
Week 2UseProcess eligible purchases. Record all manual work outside Axora.
Week 3StressTest rejected delivery, cash discrepancy, cancellation and missing item.
Week 4-5RepeatReduce reminders. Measure voluntary use and repeat cycles.
Week 6DecideCompare baseline, interview users and apply go/no-go gates.

Minimum transaction scope

  • At least 10 completed eligible purchase cycles per company.
  • At least two requesters and one approver per company where practical.
  • At least one real quotation comparison per company.
  • At least one delivery exception across the pilot.
  • At least one rejected/cancelled delivery or controlled cash-discrepancy drill.

Controlled boundaries

  • Invitation-only and allowlisted users.
  • Private-sector B2B purchases only.
  • Approved low-risk categories and suppliers.
  • Per-order and per-company spend caps.
  • Axora never collects or holds COD cash; no credit or non-COD payment method.
Do not change the target during the pilotFix critical defects immediately, but record enhancement requests instead of rebuilding the product every day. Stable scope is necessary for a fair before/after comparison.

Interview guide

Ask about real behavior

Interview users before, during and after the pilot

Before

Understand the current process

  1. Tell me about the last purchase from request to delivery and COD receipt.
  2. Who touched it, and what did each person do?
  3. Which messages, sheets and files were used?
  4. Where did it wait or require follow-up?
  5. What mistake or missing document happens most often?

During

Observe friction

  1. What did you complete without help?
  2. What action was unclear or slower?
  3. What work still happened outside Axora?
  4. Which notification or status did you need?
  5. Would you use Axora if the pilot owner did not remind you?

After

Test commitment

  1. Which task became easier? Give one example.
  2. Which task did not improve?
  3. What would happen if Axora stopped tomorrow?
  4. Who should own and pay for it internally?
  5. Will your company continue for another three months?

People to include per company

  • One requester
  • One procurement/administration user
  • One approver or manager
  • One finance or receiving user

Target 3-5 participants per company, depending on the real workflow.

Evidence to retain

  • Participant role code, not unnecessary personal details
  • Date and pilot week
  • Short factual notes and one exact example
  • Rating: easier, same or harder
  • Continuation decision and conditions
Questions to avoid"Do you like Axora?", "Would you use a modern system?" and "Is automation good?" invite polite answers. Ask about the last real transaction, observed behavior and a specific continuation commitment.
Willingness-to-pay ruleA free pilot tests use, not pricing. If no fee is charged, request a signed continuation statement with an acceptable price range or a paid extension decision.

Dual-source core items

For each frequent item/category, prepare two approved suppliers so one failure does not stop the pilot and quotation comparison remains meaningful.

Do not promise national speed yetPublish only lead times demonstrated during the pilot. Measure on-time-in-full performance before advertising any service-level promise.
Manual verification is appropriate for three companies and a small supplier set.Axora | Operations

Decision after six weeks
24
Interpret three-company evidence correctly

Make a company-level decision, then decide the next test

Go

Closed beta

  • At least 2/3 companies show repeat use and measurable value.
  • COD-paid orders reconcile correctly.
  • No critical privacy/security issue.
  • At least 2 companies want to continue.

Next: improve tenant portal and recruit 5-10 more committed companies.

Improve

Focused second cycle

  • Problem is real but one workflow blocks adoption.
  • Users need a specific role, notification or report.
  • COD operations create too much manual work.
  • Value is uneven between companies.

Next: fix one evidence-backed gap and repeat for 2-4 weeks.

Stop/pivot

Do not scale

  • Use occurs only when forced.
  • No material time or effort improvement.
  • Existing tools are preferred.
  • COD cash/security/compliance risk is disproportionate.

Next: retain internal tool value or change target problem.
